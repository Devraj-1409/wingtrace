WingTrace

About

Live air traffic on a realistic 3D Earth. A free, non-commercial hobby project.

How it works
Live positions
Aircraft broadcast where they are (ADS-B). Volunteers' receivers share it as open data.
Routes
Matched by callsign from crowd-sourced data, shown only when the aircraft's position fits.
Estimated positions
Out of receiver range (usually over oceans), cruising aircraft keep moving along their route, drawn faded.

Privacy

Aircraft whose owners have asked not to be tracked publicly (the FAA LADD list and Privacy ICAO Addresses) are never shown, and military aircraft are hidden. To have an aircraft removed, contact the site owner.

Not for navigation. Information may be delayed, incomplete or wrong; never use it for flight planning or anything safety-related.

Altitude scale

Auto shows true altitude up close and lifts routes as you zoom out.
